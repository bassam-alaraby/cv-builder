Before you print

Set these in the browser's print dialog. They keep the PDF looking like the preview.

  1. Destination: Save as PDF
  2. Margins: Default — the template sets them
  3. Scale: 100%
  4. Headers and footers: off
  5. Background graphics: on

How it works

  1. Fill in the form. Everything is saved in this browser as you type, so you can close the tab and come back later.
  2. The page on the right is the real thing: it is exactly what the PDF will look like. Click any word in it to jump back to the field that writes it.
  3. Press Print / Save as PDF, choose Save as PDF as the destination, and the PDF lands in your downloads folder.

There is no account and no server. Your CV never leaves your device.
